Meridian, Idaho, USA

Location

Meridian is located about 20 miles West of Boise, Idaho.

About

History

Meridian was established in 1891 and was originally named Hunter.

Population

Meridian has a population around 148K.

Weather

Meridian weather varies greatly, from cold winters to hot and dry summers.
